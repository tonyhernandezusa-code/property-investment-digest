How These Numbers Are Used

Growth

Revenue growth and EPS growth compare the latest reported fiscal year with the prior reported fiscal year when both are available.

Profitability

Net margin equals reported annual net income divided by reported annual revenue.

Cash Generation

Free cash flow is modeled as reported operating cash flow minus reported capital expenditures when both facts are available.

Balance Sheet

Liabilities-to-assets and equity-to-assets compare the latest available balance-sheet facts filed in a 10-Q or 10-K.

Important Limitations

SEC XBRL facts come from issuer filings. Companies can use different tags, definitions, fiscal calendars and amended filings. A missing value means the updater could not identify a comparable standardized fact; it does not necessarily mean the company has no such item.

This page provides factual research and transparent calculations. It does not rate a stock as a buy, sell, or hold and does not forecast investment returns.
