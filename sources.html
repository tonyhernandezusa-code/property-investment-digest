Required record for every source

Official terms page, data fields, plan and cost, public-display rights, paid-subscriber rights, storage and caching rights, derivative-analysis rights, report rights, attribution, update frequency, written confirmation, contact person, and permission date.

Migration 4 published dataset

The State Market Research page uses summaries calculated from the county-level records already embedded in the protected County Investment Map. It introduces no additional private vendor.

Population and housing are aggregated totals. Dollar figures and unemployment are weighted county averages and are clearly labeled as such.

Migration 5: Census API automation

The automated updater retrieves 2024 ACS 5-Year state data and compares it with the non-overlapping 2019 ACS 5-Year vintage. It publishes aggregate statistics and transparent ratios.

Required notice: This product uses the Census Bureau Data API but is not endorsed or certified by the Census Bureau.

Migration 6: BLS and FHFA

BLS LAUS: Seasonally adjusted monthly state unemployment-rate series retrieved through the official BLS Public Data API.

FHFA HPI®: Seasonally adjusted quarterly purchase-only state index downloaded from the official FHFA HPI dataset page.

The updater records each source's latest period and attribution in the published JSON and source register.

Migration 7: HUD FMR and OpenFEMA

HUD FY 2026 Fair Market Rents: Official county/town downloadable records for efficiency through four-bedroom gross-rent benchmarks.

OpenFEMA Disaster Declarations Summaries: Unique federal declarations summarized by state for a rolling ten-year window. County-level duplicate rows are consolidated by disaster number.

Neither source is presented as a property recommendation, current asking rent, parcel-level hazard score, flood determination, or insurance estimate.

Migration 8: Hotel Industry Government Data

Census County Business Patterns: NAICS 721110 — Hotels (except Casino Hotels) and Motels. The site uses aggregate establishment, employment and annual-payroll records for the United States, states and counties.

Not provided by CBP: room count, occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, hotel property values, individual hotel revenue, or hotel forecasts.

Additional tourism context: the National Travel and Tourism Office publishes overseas visitor and hotel-motel traveler research. These reports are linked for research but are not converted into automated local ADR or occupancy estimates.
