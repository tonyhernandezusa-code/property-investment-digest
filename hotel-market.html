What is not included in this free government dataset?

Market occupancy, ADR, RevPAR, room inventory, individual hotel revenue, property sale prices, and hotel forecasts are not supplied by County Business Patterns.

Those operating measures can be entered manually in the Hotel Investment Analysis calculator. Commercial market data can be added later only after publication and subscriber-use rights are confirmed in writing.
