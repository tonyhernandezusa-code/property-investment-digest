Learning Center

Plain-language explanations of common measurements used to analyze direct real-estate investments.

NOI

Net operating income is effective property income minus operating expenses, before loan payments and income taxes.

Cap Rate

NOI divided by property price. It compares the property’s unleveraged operating income with its price.

DSCR

Debt-service coverage ratio is NOI divided by annual debt service.

Cash-on-Cash Return

Annual pre-tax cash flow divided by the cash invested.

Break-even Occupancy

The occupancy level generally needed for income to cover operating expenses and debt service.

Scenario Analysis

Changing rent, vacancy, expenses, interest rates, or reserves shows sensitivity to different conditions.

Debt Yield

NOI divided by the loan amount. It measures the property's income relative to the lender's exposure and does not depend on the interest rate or amortization schedule.

Gross Rent Multiplier

Purchase price divided by annual gross scheduled rent. It is a quick comparison measure that does not account for expenses, vacancy, or financing.

Equity Multiple

Total projected cash distributions divided by the initial cash invested. It does not show how quickly the distributions occur.

IRR

Internal rate of return is a time-sensitive estimate based on projected cash flows and a future sale assumption. It is highly dependent on the assumptions entered.

Scheduled vs. Occupied Rent

Scheduled rent assumes every listed unit pays its stated rent. Occupied rent includes only unit rows marked occupied. An additional vacancy allowance may be applied for collection loss or turnover.

Property Equity

Current estimated property value minus the current loan balance. Both inputs must be independently verified, and an estimated value is not an appraisal.

Local Browser Storage

The Property Manager saves data on the current device and browser. It is not a cloud account. Exporting regular backup files protects against browser-data loss.

Weighted County Average

A state comparison formed by combining county values and giving larger counties more weight. It is not the same as an official statewide median.

Permits per 1,000 Housing Units

New housing units authorized relative to the existing housing stock. This makes differently sized states easier to compare.

Home-Value-to-Income Ratio

The weighted county home-value figure divided by weighted county household income. It gives broad context but does not determine affordability for a particular household.

ACS 5-Year Estimate

A survey estimate combining five years of responses. It provides broad geographic coverage but is not a live monthly market measurement.

Non-Overlapping Comparison

Comparing 2020–2024 with 2015–2019 avoids reusing the same survey years in both periods.

Source Permission Register

A record showing which sources are active, planned, free, paid, or awaiting written permission.
