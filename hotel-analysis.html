Definitions

ADR: Room revenue divided by occupied room nights.

RevPAR: Room revenue divided by available room nights.

TRevPAR: Total hotel revenue divided by available room nights.

GOPPAR: Simplified gross operating profit divided by available room nights.

NOI: In this educational model, simplified GOP less property taxes, insurance, FF&E reserve and other fixed charges, before debt service and income taxes.

Price per key: Purchase price divided by room count.

DSCR: NOI divided by annual debt service.

Debt yield: NOI divided by loan amount.

Balloon payment: The unpaid principal balance due when the loan term ends before the stated amortization period has fully paid off the loan.

Adjustable / floating rate: A loan whose interest rate can change after an initial fixed period or at stated reset intervals. This model uses user-entered index, margin, floor, cap and rate-change assumptions.

Educational model only. This is not a USALI-compliant hotel accounting statement, appraisal, forecast, financing commitment, investment recommendation, or substitute for property-specific due diligence.
