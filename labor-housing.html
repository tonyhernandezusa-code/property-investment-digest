Source interpretation

BLS unemployment: Seasonally adjusted monthly state rates from the Local Area Unemployment Statistics program. The most recent observation may be preliminary and subject to revision.

FHFA HPI: Seasonally adjusted quarterly purchase-only state indexes based on single-family sales transactions financed through the government-sponsored enterprises. Index changes do not represent the appreciation of every property.
