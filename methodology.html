State Market Research methodology

State totals are aggregated from the county records used by the County Investment Map. Growth rates compare aggregated current and comparison-period totals. Dollar figures are weighted averages of county medians, and unemployment is population-weighted. The ranking table orders one selected measurement at a time and does not create an overall “best market” score.

Census ACS automated calculations

Migration 5 compares 2020–2024 ACS 5-Year estimates with 2015–2019 ACS 5-Year estimates. Vacancy rate equals vacant units divided by housing units. Owner and renter shares use occupied tenure counts. Home-value-to-income and annual-rent-to-income are transparent ratios based on reported ACS medians.

BLS and FHFA calculations

The unemployment-rate change is the latest seasonally adjusted state rate minus the rate for the same month one year earlier, expressed in percentage points. FHFA quarter, one-year, and five-year changes are calculated from the seasonally adjusted purchase-only index. The since-1991 figure compares the latest index with the 1991 base of 100.

HUD and FEMA calculations

State HUD summaries use the median, minimum, and maximum of the listed county/town FMR records within each state. They are not population-weighted statewide FMR estimates. The annual two-bedroom-FMR-to-income ratio divides twelve times the median listed two-bedroom FMR by ACS median household income.

FEMA summaries count each state/disaster-number combination once because the source contains separate rows for declared counties and other geographic areas. The rolling window begins January 1 of the year ten years before the update year.

Hotel analysis methodology

The Hotel Investment Analysis is a transparent educational model using user-entered occupancy, ADR, revenue, expense and financing assumptions. RevPAR equals room revenue divided by available room nights. TRevPAR equals total revenue divided by available room nights. The model's simplified GOP subtracts variable expenses, fixed/semi-fixed operating expenses, franchise fees and management fees from total revenue. NOI then subtracts property taxes, insurance, the FF&E reserve and other fixed charges before debt service and income taxes.

The pro forma compounds the user's ADR, other-revenue and expense growth assumptions and changes occupancy by the stated annual percentage-point assumption. The illustrative exit value uses Year 11 NOI divided by the user-entered exit cap rate. It is not an appraisal or forecast.

Hotel market methodology

Census County Business Patterns records are filtered to NAICS 721110, legal form 001 (all establishments) and employment-size class 001 (all establishments). Current 2023 results are compared with 2019. Growth percentages are calculated from the published aggregate counts.
