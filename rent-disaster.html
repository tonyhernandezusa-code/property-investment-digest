Important distinctions

HUD FMR: A gross-rent benchmark used for federal housing programs. It includes shelter rent and specified tenant-paid utilities, and it is not a live market-rent quote.

FEMA declaration history: A record of federal declarations. More declarations can reflect exposure, event frequency, declaration practices, geography, or other factors. It is not a property-risk score.
